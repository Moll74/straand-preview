The season at Straand

1 May

Start the season off right! On 1 May, motorcycle and car enthusiasts gather at Straand when we open the roof terrace for Cars, Bikes & Coffee – with good coffee, tasty lunch dishes and the hum of engines as a backdrop.

Rustløser riding refresher

On the second weekend in May, a Rustløser riding refresher course for motorcyclists is arranged at Fyresdal airfield. Welcome to a safe and inspiring start to the season – where you can brush up on your skills and the joy of riding.

Straandtreffet

Welcome to Straandtreffet – the highlight of the year for motorcycle enthusiasts, always the 3rd weekend in September. Book a room and join an unforgettable end of the season in Telemark.

Nordic Triumph Treff 2027

Welcome to the Triumph Nordic Treff 2027, 13–15 August! Join us for a weekend filled with the joy of riding and great fellowship, hosted by Triumph Motorcycles Nordic in collaboration with LEKS Motor Oslo, MC Tuning in Trondheim, Bikelife Norge, Straand Hotel and Hubriding.

HubRiding

Hubriding is a concept where you stay at the hotel and experience new, exciting driving routes every single day. Developed by us at Straand Hotel together with our colleagues at De Historiske. Explore more at hubriding.no.

Bikers Welcome

Straand Hotel is Norway's first Bikers Welcome hotel. Read the review at Visit Telemark and discover why we are a natural choice for motorcycle enthusiasts. With us, everything is in place for good riding experiences – with adapted routes, facilities and a genuine passion for motorcycle life.

The motorcycle spring ride-out (MC-vårslippet): the traditional start to the motorcycle season in Vestfold and Telemark, with driving pleasure, community and traffic safety in focus. Vrådal is filled with motorcycle enthusiasts from all over the country.

About the riding refresher

Stay here, ride new routes every day

HubRiding means that you stay at Straand Hotel and choose from many defined driving routes in the surrounding area. New experiences every day — without moving your gear.

  • Tens of thousands of bends in Norway's best driving terrain
  • Spectacular mountain passes and panoramic views
  • Asphalt and gravel — routes for all types of vehicles
  • Local eateries along the routes
  • New routes are published regularly on hubriding.no

A stay for those who live for the joy of driving

Welcome to a stay where you can pull the cover off the vehicle, turn the key and feel the good feeling again – the first drive of the year. Whether you’re behind the handlebars of your motorcycle or behind the wheel of a well-maintained classic, Telemark’s most beautiful roads await you just outside the hotel.

With us, you will meet others who live for the same thing. People who know what a good bend is worth, who appreciate the sound of a well-tuned engine, and who share stories over a hot cup of coffee after the day’s ride. New acquaintances, old friends – and a shared joy for vehicles and roads.

Let us spoil you with good home-made food, comfortable rooms and a base in the middle of Norway’s eldorado for vehicles. The Tivoli bends, the HubRiding routes and the spectacular mountain passes are ready. So are we.

Take advantage of the long weekends to get out on the road:

  • Whitsun weekend: 22–25 May

The roof terrace is open — stop by!

Are you out on a day trip and just want to take a breather? Our roof terrace is open both Ascension weekend and Whitsun weekend – also for those who are not staying overnight. Stop by for a hot cup of coffee, a cold refreshment or a bite to eat with a view of Nisser. You are free to park your vehicle on the square, sit down and have a chat with other enthusiasts.

Telemarksvegen, RV41

The main road to Vrådal and West Telemark from southern Norway is Telemarksvegen, RV41, which runs through scenic surroundings from Kristiansand along the Tovdal river and the Nidelva river up to Treungen, where it follows Nisser further up to Vrådal and the E134 at Brunkeberg.

Along the way you will find rest areas with posts carved as decorations from local cultural monuments: stave churches, ølhøner (traditional carved ale bowls) and rose painting.

Vrådal and your hosts

Autumn is one of the loveliest seasons in Vrådal. With us you can stay and recharge, enjoy time with family and old and new friends, and keep yourself and others entertained with a wide range of activities. We have literally something for every taste.

Vrådal is a small, idyllic village in West Telemark, surrounded by fantastic nature and a beautiful lake. For five generations we as a family have run, built and developed the hotel for the enjoyment of our guests. We have established our own traditions and our own identity, and today we are proud members of De Historiske.

As a guest you have our full attention and priority. Our offer is presented as three pillars in one HUB, giving you an overview of and access to all of it.
