New experiences winter 2026

Alpine skiing and cross-country skiing never go out of style, but in the winter of 2026 you can try some brand new experiences in Vrådal! How about a moonlight tour in the mountains (Stargazing)? Or the feeling of being served a steaming hot campfire coffee after snowshoeing in beautiful nature (Forest Barista)? In Trapper’s Cabin you will be served moose stew in our charming storehouse, while we can offer fondue for the whole family inside the hotel. Read more and choose your new winter experience!

Forest Barista

Mondays. Put on snowshoes, hike up the mountainside, light a fire and make coffee on a kettle under the open sky. Nature is your café.

NOK 295,- per person

Trapper's Cabin

Tuesdays. Dinner in Norway’s smallest inn! The old storehouse is furnished as a cozy trapper’s cabin with steaming hot game stew.

NOK 495,- per person

Stargazing

Wednesdays. Join us for a magical evening walk under the starry sky. Vrådal’s dark skies are perfect for discovering the stars.

NOK 295,- per person

Fondue

Thursdays. A cozy and social fondue experience with delicious flavors for young and old. Share the food, enjoy the company.

NOK 495,- per person

Alpine & Skiing

Ski days that give peace of mind

The winter holiday at Straand is all about the perfect balance between activity and tranquility. Start the day out in fresh mountain air – with alpine skiing in Vrådal Panorama or a leisurely cross-country skiing trip in snow-covered nature. Whether you like speed on the slopes or prefer the silence of the trail, this is the place for true winter fun.

When you return, the heat awaits. Relax in the hotel’s heated pool and sauna, or make the experience extra special with the wood-fired sauna in Vrådal Panorama. For those who want to feel the contrasts: finish with a refreshing ice bath – and enter the evening with new energy.

Welcome to the mountains - welcome to Telemark!

Telemark SuperSki is Norway’s largest ski region with five ski centers – Gausta, Rauland, Vrådal, Haukeli and Lifjell – offering everything from family-friendly slopes to some of the country’s best off-piste areas. Here you will find great variety, snow-sure conditions and a complete offer for the whole family. With one lift pass, you get access to over 120 slopes, 54 lifts and more than 450 kilometers of cross-country ski trails.

Try winter in magical Vrådal

Welcome to our winter paradise!

When you walk through the doors with us at the historic Straand Hotel in Vrådal, you are already in wonderland. Book your winter stay for the whole family now!

We at Straand Hotel have been welcoming guests since 1864, and we are happy to state: Vrådal is a very cozy place to spend your family holiday! Here you get everything from alpine slopes with café and sauna at the top, ski slopes in the forest, adventurous nature, fireplace with something hot in your cup in our historic living rooms as well as a swimming pool with sauna and exciting play opportunities for the children! We are happy to transport you to the ski center with our ski bus.
