Christmas parties at Straand Hotel 2026

Six weekends before Christmas. Home-made Christmas food in the historic lounges, with a show and entertainment. Rooms are booked separately.

Delicious food, shows and pre-Christmas atmosphere in historic surroundings.

Tradition since 1864

Host the Christmas table at Straand Hotel

At Straand, we have extensive experience in arranging Christmas parties. Come and experience the pre-Christmas atmosphere in our historic living rooms and enjoy delicious homemade food with an emphasis on local and good ingredients.

True to tradition, we have shows and entertainment as part of the Christmas party. You can also book team building activities for your group!

13 – 15 Nov · Christmas party with Quizgutta

Weekend 1 · Quiz. Join a Christmas party evening out of the ordinary – where good food, laughter and a healthy dose of competitive spirit go hand in hand. Quizgutta take over the stage and guide you through an evening packed with questions, music and fun across all genres. Gather the gang and find out who really knows their Christmas. We serve a traditional Christmas party, a lively atmosphere and an evening you will be talking about long after the last question has been asked. Tickets are coming.

Book accommodation

20 – 22 Nov · (H)julebord for motoring enthusiasts

Weekend 2 · Engine + Christmas party. Straand Hotel, AMCAR and motoring friends from all over the country invite you to a Christmas party out of the ordinary – for those with petrol in their blood and the joy of creating in their souls. We offer a tasting of Straand’s self-developed HubRiding Afterdrive aquavit, the creative session Sylinder Summing, a traditional Christmas party, and live music with Vandringsmenn. A new meeting place for motor enthusiasts on 2 and 4 wheels – and the start of new ideas in 2026.
