Fram Bar

Fram Bar is our warm meeting place on Straand, inspired by the veteran boat MS Fram, which since 1909 has been part of the history of Nisser and Vråvatn. Here you can enjoy a good cup of coffee, a glass of wine or one of our signature cocktails, perhaps after a day filled with experiences – or just because the evening deserves a little extra time together.

A place for good conversations, small moments and something good in the glass.

Straand. More than food and drink.

Part of the experience.

At Straand, it’s not just about what is served on the plate or in the glass. It’s about the feeling of gathering around a good meal, sharing stories over a cup of coffee, toasting small and big moments and creating memories that last long after the holiday is over.

We love to give our guests that little extra attention. From warm smiles and personal service to tasty dishes made with care, carefully selected wines and refreshing drinks enjoyed with a view of beautiful Nisser. Whether you come as a couple, family, friends or colleagues, we want to create experiences that bring people together around the table.

And while we can tell a lot in words, sometimes it’s the pictures that tell the story best.

About Skjusstasjonen Pub

When are breakfast, lunch and dinner served?

Breakfast from 8 am. Lunch 12–6 pm in the piano bar or on the terrace. Dinner has seatings between 7 and 9 pm, so tell us your preferred time.

Can I eat at the hotel without staying?

Yes, everyone is welcome. Call +47 35 06 90 00 to book a table.

Do you cater for allergies and gluten-free diets?

Yes. Several dishes can be served with gluten-free bread. Tell us about allergies when you book a table or on arrival.

Do you serve afternoon tea?

Yes, 12–5 pm in our historic lounges.

When are the bar and terrace open?

Fram Bar opens at noon, with the bar menu until 9 pm. The terrace opens at noon with food until 6 pm, weather permitting.

When is the beach bar open?

The beach bar on Lake Nisser is open approx. 23 June–20 August.
