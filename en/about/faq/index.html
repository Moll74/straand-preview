Booking & rates

How do I book a room?

Choose your dates and number of guests in the booking bar at the top of the page to see available rooms and today's rates. You can also call reception on +47 35 06 90 00.

How much does a room cost?

The price depends on dates, room type and package. You see all rates when you choose dates in the booking.

Is breakfast included?

It is shown in the rate you choose in the booking. Breakfast can also be bought at the hotel.

How do I cancel or change my booking?

Bookings made directly with us can be changed or cancelled until 2 pm the day before arrival. For some events, such as Christmas and New Year, the deadline is two weeks. A no-show is charged for the first night.

When do I pay?

On arrival. At check-in we take an authorisation on your card, so the amount is reserved until check-out. If you pay cash, we need a card as a guarantee.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, Eurocard, Diners, JCB, BankAxept and Vipps. The hotel does not exchange currency, and cash withdrawals are not possible.

Can I pay by invoice?

Invoicing must be agreed before the stay, requires a credit check and applies to stays over NOK 5,000. We do not invoice private individuals.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards can be used for an overnight stay with breakfast, dinner in the restaurant, an aperitif with storytelling, the Straand burger and pizza, and cabin or apartment stays.

Can I stay if I am under 18?

No, nobody under 18 can book or stay alone. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by an adult who is responsible during the stay.

Arrival & departure

When are check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 3 pm, from 4 pm in June–September. Check-out before 11 am.

Can I check in early?

If your room is ready, you check in straight away. Otherwise you can use all the facilities, such as the pool and restaurant, until it is.

Can I check out late?

Yes, if we have capacity. Arrange it at reception the evening before at the latest. It costs NOK 100 per room per extra hour.

Is reception open 24 hours?

Yes, reception is open around the clock on +47 35 06 90 00.

Is there parking, and what does it cost?

Parking is free. The hotel's outdoor car park has approx. 100 spaces for cars and motorcycles, right next to the hotel.

Can I charge my electric car?

Yes. There are EV chargers at the hotel, both for hotel guests and Charge365 chargers paid in the app.

Rooms

What room types do you have?

Historic rooms, standard doubles, family rooms, demi-suites, accessible rooms and singles: 125 rooms in total.

What is in the rooms?

All rooms have TV and wifi, and a bathroom with shower or bath, toilet and hairdryer. The demi-suites also have a coffee machine and a balcony.

Do you have wheelchair-accessible rooms?

We have two rooms with wide doors, a large bathroom and room for a wheelchair, and some family rooms can also be used. If you need a shower chair or raised toilet seat, let us know in good time.

Do you have allergy-friendly rooms?

Yes, the hotel has allergy rooms. Let us know when you book.

Can I bring my dog or other pets?

Yes, but tell us in advance, as only some rooms allow pets. The fee is NOK 300 per room per stay. Pets are not allowed in the shared areas, and you get your own entrance. Service and guide dogs are exempt.

Is the hotel non-smoking?

Straand is a non-smoking hotel. Smoking in the room is charged NOK 1,500, but there are outdoor areas where smoking is allowed.

Do you have cabins or apartments?

Yes, through Vrådal Booking we rent out cabins and holiday apartments in Vrådal for self-catering stays.

Food & drink

When are breakfast, lunch and dinner served?

Breakfast from 8 am. Lunch 12–6 pm in the piano bar or on the terrace. Dinner has seatings between 7 and 9 pm, so tell us your preferred time.

Can I eat at the hotel without staying?

Yes, everyone is welcome. Call +47 35 06 90 00 to book a table.

Do you cater for allergies and gluten-free diets?

Yes. Several dishes can be served with gluten-free bread. Tell us about allergies when you book a table or on arrival.

Do you serve afternoon tea?

Yes, 12–5 pm in our historic lounges.

When are the bar and terrace open?

Fram Bar opens at noon, with the bar menu until 9 pm. The terrace opens at noon with food until 6 pm, weather permitting.

When is the beach bar open?

The beach bar on Lake Nisser is open approx. 23 June–20 August.

What is "the taste of Telemark"?

It is the kitchen's idea: local, seasonal produce from around us, such as game, berries and mushrooms in autumn.

Facilities & wellness

Is there a pool and sauna?

Yes. The hotel has a heated indoor pool, open 7 am–7 pm. The saunas are open 7–9 am and 4–7 pm.

Can day visitors use the pool?

Yes, both day visitors and hotel guests can use the pool, the playground and the beach.

Is there free wifi?

Yes, free wifi throughout the hotel.

Do you have a gym?

Yes, the hotel has a fitness room.

Families & children

Is Straand good for families?

Yes. We have family rooms, a playroom with Nintendo Wii and board games, and in summer a beach, trampolines and water activities at Straand Sommerland.

What can children do when it rains?

Swim in the indoor pool, play in the playroom or borrow toy boxes and board games.

What is Straand Sommerland?

Our own beach club on Lake Nisser, with a sandy beach, swimming, SUP, kayaks, canoes, pedal boats, trampolines, beach volleyball and kubb, and a beach bar with simple summer food.

Activities & experiences

What is MS Fram?

The hotel's vintage boat from 1909. It sails on Lake Nisser and Vråvatn through the Småstraum and Storstraum locks, from mid-May to early October.

Can we eat on board MS Fram?

Coffee, drinks and snacks are served on board. Fresh baguettes or tapas platters can be pre-ordered.

Can I hire a canoe, kayak or bike?

Yes. Canoes, kayaks, bikes and e-bikes are hired at reception or at the Straand Sommerland bar. Life jackets are required by law, and you can hire one for NOK 50.

Can I play golf nearby?

Yes, on the 9-hole course on Fossøy, an island in Lake Nisser close to the hotel. The hotel rents out golf carts (the renter must be over 18).

Where can we go hiking?

Vrådal has marked routes for families and experienced hikers. Venelifjellet (905 m) has panoramic views, and Gaustatoppen makes a great day trip.

Are you motorcycle friendly?

Yes, Straand was Norway's first Bikers Welcome hotel and created HUBRiding: 15 routes with guaranteed bends, free parking, vehicle washing and gear drying.

What is there to do in Vrådal in winter?

Ski slopes, forest ski trails, fireside evenings, the pool and sauna, and play for the children.

Meetings, celebrations & events

Can we hold a meeting or conference with you?

Yes. The Vrådal Hall is about 660 m² and seats 400 in one room. It can be divided, and there are several smaller rooms and breakout rooms.

What equipment do the meeting rooms have?

All meeting rooms have a PA system, screen and flip chart, and the breakout rooms have a screen and flip chart. Wifi throughout, and a 4.5-metre drive-in door straight into the hall.

Can you arrange team building and activities?

Yes, for example guided mountain hikes, canoe trips, trail biking, climbing, snowshoe and ski trips, ski instruction and team-building competitions.

Can we have our wedding at Straand?

Yes. A ceremony in the apple orchard, a party in historic rooms, a vintage car to the church and MS Fram for up to 90 guests.

Do you host Christmas parties?

Yes, on six weekends before Christmas, with home-made Christmas food in the historic lounges and a show. Rooms are booked separately from shows and events.

What is the Straand Festival?

The annual music and dance festival with Norwegian dance music on several stages, on the weekend of 30 October–1 November.

Location & contact

Where is the hotel?

Kviteseidvegen 1698, 3853 Vrådal, at the northern end of Lake Nisser in West Telemark.

How far is it from Oslo?

Vrådal is in West Telemark, a natural stop between Oslo and Bergen. Kristiansand and Larvik are 2–3 hours away by car, Stavanger and Bergen 5–6 hours. Getting here has directions from Oslo.

Can I get to the hotel by bus?

Yes, the bus stop "Straand" is right next to the hotel. Check routes and times with Entur (entur.no).

How do I contact you?

Call +47 35 06 90 00 (reception is open 24 hours) or email straand@straand.no.

History & sustainability

How old is Straand Hotel?

Straand became a coaching inn in 1864 and has been run by the same family for five generations. Today Katrine and Lars Arne Straand are the hosts.

What is De Historiske?

An association of historic hotels and restaurants in Norway. Straand Hotel is a member.

What does the hotel do for the environment?

Straand is Eco-Lighthouse certified, uses local produce, reduces food waste by serving plated meals most of the year, and takes part in making the region a sustainable destination.
